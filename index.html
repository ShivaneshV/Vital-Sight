Healthcare that follows you home.

VitalSight connects patients with their clinical care teams through seamless, automatic remote patient monitoring (RPM). Track blood pressure, weight, and critical signs with zero tech barriers.

Get Started Learn More

Continuous care, made simple.

Our HIPAA-compliant connected medical equipment automatically transmits measurements to your care team without requiring home Wi-Fi or smartphone pairings.

🩸

Connected Blood Pressure

Clinically validated blood pressure cuffs automatically record and transmit systolic, diastolic, and pulse measurements instantly.

⚖️

Smart Weight Telemetry

Cellular-connected weight scales log changes in fluid retention and body mass, helping doctors manage heart health proactively.

📡

Zero-Config Data Hub

No phone pairing or Wi-Fi configuration required. Simply plug in the pre-configured hub, and the devices transmit securely out-of-the-box.

📊

EMR Integration

Vital measurements sync directly with major Electronic Medical Record (EMR) systems, allowing doctors to inspect logs inside their existing workflow.

Empowering patients and clinics alike.

By bringing regular health tracking into the home, VitalSight helps prevent costly emergency visits and supports proactive clinical adjustments.

  • ✔

    Zero Technology Barriers

  • ✔

    Know that a clinician is reviewing your measurements regularly and will reach out if readings exceed set limits.

  • ✔

🩺 For Providers

  • ✔
